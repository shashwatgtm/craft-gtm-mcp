Customer Interview Kit

CHURN Interview

Target Persona: Head of Sales Operations

Product Context: Field sales app for consumer goods brands and their distributors

Industry: enterprise software

Complexity Level: moderate

Sector: read from your inputs as vertical SaaS. Business model: software subscription (read from your inputs; set Business model to change it).

Pre-Interview Checklist
  • [ ] Reviewed persona's LinkedIn/background
  • [ ] Tested recording equipment
  • [ ] Prepared note-taking template
  • [ ] Sent calendar invite with clear agenda
  • [ ] Confirmed interview timing (45-60 min recommended)
  • [ ] Prepared incentive (if applicable)
Interview Objectives
  1. Understand the current reality and challenges of: Head of Sales Operations
  2. Understand true reasons for leaving
  3. Gather stories and quotes for internal use
Opening (5 min)

Build rapport before diving into questions

  • I appreciate you taking the time despite deciding to leave. Your feedback is invaluable.
  • Can you tell me about your experience with us overall?
  • When did you first start thinking about leaving?

Industry-specific opener: "How do you measure secondary sales today, and how much does it vary?"

Core Questions (25-30 min)
Main Line of Inquiry
  1. What was the primary reason you decided to cancel?
  2. Were there any secondary reasons?
  3. What did you switch to? What made that option better?
  4. Was there anything we could have done to keep you?
  5. Did you feel you got value from the product?
Moderate-Level Technical Questions
  1. Walk me through your typical workflow with the product.
  2. What integrations are most important to you?
  3. How do you measure success with tools like this?
Industry-Specific Questions (vertical SaaS)
  1. How do your reps capture orders in the outlet today, and how does that reach the distributor?
  2. How do you see secondary sales by outlet and by SKU today, and how late is that view?
  3. What share of your outlets does a rep cover in a typical beat, and who plans the beats?
  4. How are trade schemes communicated to reps and checked at the outlet?
  5. Which distributor systems (DMS) and ERP must the sales app work with?
  6. How do National Sales Head, Head of Distribution and Regional Sales Manager take part in this decision, and who has the final say?

Who usually decides in this sector: The National Sales Head or Managing Director signs; the Head of Sales Operations champions; regional managers and field reps use it; distributors hold the stock and order data; IT checks the ERP and DMS integration.

Words this buyer uses: distributor, outlet, beat plan, secondary sales, general trade, SKU, order capture, retail execution, DMS, trade scheme. Use them where they are true for the person you interview.

Probing Questions (Use as needed)

Follow the energy: when they light up or seem frustrated, probe deeper

  • They gave a main reason for leaving. Ask: "When did that first become a problem?"
  • Did you reach out to support about this? What happened?
  • What would have needed to change for you to stay?
  • How did you make the final decision? Who was involved?
  • Looking back, what should we have done differently?

Silence technique: After an answer, wait 3-5 seconds. They often add the most valuable insights in the silence.

Closing (5 min)
  • Is there any scenario where you'd consider coming back?
  • What should we tell other customers who have similar concerns?
  • Is there anything else you want us to know?

Always end with: "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"

Who is Customer Interview Kit not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above; the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished, publish-ready plans: each tool returns a structured draft, with placeholders where your inputs gave no fact.
  • Anyone who needs live market or competitor data: the tools look nothing up; they work from what you type, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.

How do I use Customer Interview Kit from Claude?

Add CRAFT GTM once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use CRAFT GTM's Customer Interview Kit tool. interview type: Churn; product context: Field sales app for consumer goods brands and their distributors; target persona: Head of Sales Operations; industry: Enterprise software; product complexity: Moderate.

How does Customer Interview Kit work?

No sign-in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Customer Interview Kit.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT GTM code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
