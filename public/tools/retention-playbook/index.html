How do I build a B2B customer retention playbook and health score from churn reasons?

Retention Playbook compounds your monthly churn rate into annual customer loss against example severity thresholds, maps each churn reason you supply to a signal to watch, a first step, an owner, and a draft message, and sets an illustrative 0 to 100 health score across five signals (login frequency, feature adoption, support tickets, billing health, and engagement trend). Accounts sit in four example bands (Critical at 0 to 30, At Risk at 31 to 50, Monitor at 51 to 70, and Healthy at 71 to 100), paired with lifecycle touchpoints at Day 7, Day 30, Day 60, Day 90, and 60 days before renewal.

What vertical SaaS customers measure

Vertical SaaS customers judge a provider by adoption by role, time to onboard a new team, hours of admin work saved, errors and rework and data accuracy; check whether those measures moved for the customers who left. They use words such as workflow, field and office, mobile app, adoption, data migration and integration; use the same words in your messages.

How to read the answer

The playbook starts with First evidence and capacity decisions (warning you to validate reported churn causes with affected customers and keep unknown departures separate), then calculates your compounded annual customer loss under Where you stand (for example, 1.5% monthly compounds to 16.6% of customers lost in a year). Below that, it gives a play for each churn reason (Signal to watch, First step, Owner and timing, and Draft message), an illustrative 0-to-100 Health score table (marking signals "to add" if you did not name available data signals), a capacity split (20% personal, 30% one-to-many, 50% automated for a small team), a lifecycle outreach schedule (Day 7 to 60 days before renewal), and Measures to watch.

What to do next

  • Founder: Interview the last three customers who left to confirm whether the reported churn reasons match their actual reason for cancelling before rolling out automated plays.
  • CEO: Review the compounded annual churn figure under Where you stand and confirm your customer success team has the hours required for the 20% personal / 30% one-to-many / 50% automated coverage split.
  • CMO: Adapt the Draft message and lifecycle touchpoints (Day 7, Day 30, Day 60, Day 90, and 60 days before renewal) using the words your sector's buyers actually use.
  • CRO: Enforce the Day 30 First value review and 60 days before renewal executive review so renewals are anchored on measured adoption by role before procurement steps in.
  • Demand generation head: Build the automated onboarding and feature-tip nurture sequences that cover the 50% automated contact share of customer communications.

Why come back

Re-run the playbook with your Available data signals, Current interventions, and product name filled in so the health score table maps to your real product analytics, and return every quarter to update your churn rate and save rate. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.
