Partner program for our company

First referral pilot

Existing partner evidence is unknown.
Qualify one introduction: the buyer confirms an approval or workflow problem, the relevant decision role and permission for contact. Record the introduction source, date and CRM owner; agree attribution before outreach. Review the pilot after the first introduction with the partner and owner, using acceptance, buyer response and support time. Continue only when qualification and attribution are agreed.

Commercial confirmation: Commission rate and margin constraint are unconfirmed. No payment terms are proposed.

This is a agency si partner program for our company's field sales app for consumer goods brands. A typical deal is $24000 ACV. You can support partners at a moderate level.

Your stated goal: "20% of new revenue from partners"

Sector: read from your inputs as vertical SaaS, FMCG retail execution. Business model: not stated. Tell us how you charge and the plan will adjust.

Billing dependent steps are conditional: trials, seats and recurring renewals require a subscription; a statement of work requires a services contract; site or link commitments require connectivity billing. Confirm the applicable choice before using those steps.

How the program works

Services partners implement, customise and support your product, and earn implementation fees as well as referral fees.

With moderate capacity, give your top partners one to one support and put the others on self serve with office hours.

Partners That Fit This Sector

These are kinds of company, not names: pick the ones that already advise or sell to your buyers.

In vertical SaaS, FMCG retail execution, agency si partners are usually ERP and DMS integrators and change management consultancies for sales teams. The National Sales Head and Head of Sales Operations take advice from route-to-market consultants, and the rollout depends on the DMS and ERP integrators who already work with the distributors.

Between them they reach the people who decide: the National Sales Head or Managing Director signs; the Head of Sales Operations champions; regional managers and field reps use it; distributors hold the stock and order data; IT checks the ERP and DMS integration.

Your product works with sales operations, so also look for CRM and ERP integrators, route to market consultancies, and trade marketing agencies.

Words your buyers use, for partner materials: distributor, outlet, beat plan, secondary sales, general trade, SKU, order capture, retail execution, DMS, trade scheme. A joint proof point that lands: secondary sales or productive calls in the pilot region against a comparable region without the product.

Track all of them in your CRM or partner portal.

The first 90 days
  • Days 0 to 7, welcome and set up: the partner signs the agreement, gets portal access and a digital welcome kit, and has a kickoff call (where it applies).
  • Days 7 to 30, enable: certification is started or completed, sales materials are in use, and the partner walks through the objections buyers in vertical SaaS, FMCG retail execution raise: reps will not use another app, our DMS vendor bundles a sales app and distributor data is not in sync. The first prospect is identified.
  • Days 30 to 60, activate: the first deal is registered, a pipeline starts to build and a regular cadence is set.
  • Days 60 to 90, optimise: the first deal closes, you hold a performance review and discuss the expansion plan.

Our field sales app for consumer goods brands works with sales operations, which is the part your clients would use. The people who decide on this are usually National Sales Head, Head of Sales Operations and Head of Distribution. As an implementation partner you would deliver the work, supported by our team.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to explore fit?

The our company partnerships team

How to read the answer

The output starts with a First referral pilot gate and Commercial confirmation warning (Commission rate and margin constraint are unconfirmed. No payment terms are proposed), followed by How the program works, Partners That Fit This Sector, three Partner tiers (Registered, Certified, and Premier), Targets and KPIs, The first 90 days, and a Recruitment email. Every qualification count and tier structure is an example to replace with your own, and partner earnings are held at Terms for <Tier> await commercial confirmation until you verify unit cost, retained margin, and attribution window.

What to do next

  • Founder: Run the First referral pilot with one partner introduction first, agreeing on qualification criteria, CRM owner, and attribution rules before building a portal or multi-tier program.
  • CEO: Complete the Commercial terms to confirm check with finance (payment basis, unit cost, retained margin, and attribution window) before offering any referral or reseller commission rate.
  • CMO: Equip your first pilot partners with the sector buyer vocabulary and regional pilot proof point described under Partners That Fit This Sector.
  • CRO: Set up deal registration and attribution tracking in your CRM for the metrics in Targets and KPIs (Implementations delivered, Customer satisfaction, Expansion revenue influenced, and Certified consultants).
  • Demand generation head: Customize the Recruitment email with your company name and specific partner value proposition to book 15-minute exploratory calls with target agencies or integrators.

Why come back

Come back after your first partner pilot closes to add your existing partner counts, target time period, and confirmed billing model so the 90-day plan and tier targets match your live program. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.
