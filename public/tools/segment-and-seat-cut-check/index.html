Segment and Seat Cut Check

Which segment should we back, and is our pricing safe if AI cuts seats?

For founders, CEOs, CMOs, CROs and demand generation heads in B2B SaaS.

Ask it in Claude or ChatGPT. The chat asks for your numbers per segment, the server works every sum exactly, and you get one page per segment. Same numbers in, same page out, every time.

What you read first

Retention and payback, side by side, before any score. The example below is the illustrative two segment exercise from guide 29, worked by this tool from the guide's inputs. The numbers are invented for the exercise.

LineRegulatedDigital native
Payback on CAC and ARR9.0 months6.0 months
Payback on gross profit, at a 72% margin12.5 months8.3 months
Logo retention a year89%74%
Gross revenue retention92%82%
Net revenue retention108%92%
Gross retention if an additional quarter of seats is cut in accounts with 32% of opening ARR84%not tested

Payback on CAC and ARR is CAC over one month of the contract's yearly revenue. A fast payback on a segment that keeps too little revenue buys more of what leaves, so the two are read together.

One page per segment

Retention and payback

Payback months on CAC and ARR, then on gross profit. Logo, gross and net retention on the same lines for every segment.

Seller capacity

What one new seller closes in year one after ramp and the sales cycle, the sellers your target needs, and the sellers your qualified demand can carry.

Pricing unit risk

The renewal if customers cut a share of seats, what it does to gross and net retention, and the usage rate that would replace the seat revenue.

Size and motion

First drafts from the sizing calculator on ICP Intelligence and the EPIC audit on GTM Alpha, carried with a note on how to read each.

Three things you confirm before the sums

The segment split

Is this how last year's closed deals really divide? The chat must ask you first. Sums wait until the caller reports your confirmation.

Your own win rate

From your closed deals, not an example rate. Until then, every sum that needs it waits.

What customers can cut

What you bill on, what a customer can cut, and when. For retention, the cut must be additional to reported retention and the affected revenue measured against opening ARR. Net retention also needs expansion revenue held unchanged.

The server cannot verify that a person said yes. Confirmation is an instruction to the calling chat, not proof of consent. Anything you do not give reads Missing. Nothing is filled in for you, and no figure is a benchmark.

What each role does next

  • Founder: back one segment on retention and payback, and write the retention lines that release the next money.
  • CEO: decide whether the pricing unit changes, and when, before renewals arrive.
  • CMO: set one motion per segment and check it against how the last 20 deals arrived.
  • CRO: place sellers where one seller earns back its cost soonest, and hire behind the gate.
  • Demand generation head: set budget per segment from payback, and report qualified demand every month.

Rerun it each quarter with new retention numbers, and before each renewal season with a new seat cut share.
