Check product market fit.

For founders and CMOs. The decision: do we have product market fit, and is it time to scale?

Use this product market fit scorecard to review the metrics you already track for one B2B segment. How do I score B2B product-market fit from churn, NPS, LTV:CAC, and activation?

Compare your supplied metrics against labelled example benchmarks for your target market across these dimensions, each scored out of 10: Customer Retention (Churn), Customer Satisfaction (NPS), Unit Economics (LTV:CAC), Retention Rate (or Revenue Retention), and Activation (left out for services, connectivity, and investment models). PMF Scorecard averages the scored dimensions into an overall PMF score out of 10 and lists missing metrics or missing churn periods under Data Gaps to Fill.

How to read the answer

The scorecard parses the metrics you typed (such as Churn, NPS, LTV, CAC, Retention, and Activation) and scores up to five dimensions out of 10 against example benchmarks for your selected target market. The Overall PMF Assessment (for example 7.7/10, Emerging PMF) averages only the dimensions that could be scored: missing metrics (such as Activation Rate NOT PROVIDED) or metrics missing a time period (such as Churn 1.5% without monthly or annual stated) are left out of the average and listed under Data Gaps to Fill. If your selected target market differs from the sector read from your product description, a Benchmark/description mismatch warning appears at the top so you can confirm which benchmark applies.

What to do next

  • Founder: Fill the Data Gaps to Fill first (state whether churn is monthly or annual, and define and measure your activation rate) and re-run the scorecard so the overall score reflects all five dimensions.
  • CEO: Hold off on aggressive sales hiring if your stage is Emerging PMF or if Customer Retention and Activation are still unmeasured or below benchmark.
  • CMO: Follow the NPS priority action by grouping detractor comments, fixing the biggest complaint with product, and collecting a sector proof point from your promoters.
  • CRO: Check Unit Economics (LTV:CAC) and Revenue Retention against your actual sales payback period before increasing outbound quota headcount.
  • Demand generation head: Focus acquisition spend on the customer segment that reaches your activation event fastest and retains above the healthy benchmark.

Why come back

Re-run the scorecard immediately after adding your churn measurement period and activation rate, and return at the end of every quarter to track whether your retention, NPS, and unit economics are ready for scaling. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.

Who is the PMF Scorecard tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished plans ready to publish: each tool returns a structured draft, with placeholders where your inputs gave no fact.
  • Anyone who needs live market or competitor data: the tools do not look anything up. They work from what you type, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.

How does the PMF Scorecard tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run PMF Scorecard.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT GTM code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
