What is CRAFT GTM? CRAFT GTM is a free web app and AI connector with ten planning tools for founders and CMOs. It is built by Shashwat Ghosh of Helix GTM Consulting, an AI native fractional CMO who builds his own GTM tools. Give it your metrics, launch date, customers or competitors, and it returns a product market fit scorecard, a launch plan, playbooks or battle cards. To use it in a supported MCP client, add https://craft-gtm.gtmhelix.com/mcp; you do not need to sign in.

Who is CRAFT GTM not ideal for?

  • Teams that want finished plans ready to publish: each tool returns a structured draft, with placeholders where your inputs gave no fact.
  • Anyone who needs live market or competitor data: the tools do not look anything up. They work from what you type, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.
  • Teams that need saved history or shared workspaces: answers are not stored, so keep each result where you keep your work.

How do I use CRAFT GTM?

  • In your browser: open a tool above and press Fill in an example to see how it works, then run it with your own details.
  • In supported MCP clients: add CRAFT GTM once as a custom connector (there is no sign in), then ask in plain words. The Use it in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini page has the steps.

Questions people ask

Is there a free MCP server or AI tool for B2B go to market planning?

Yes. CRAFT GTM, by Helix GTM Consulting, has ten go to market planning tools: a paid acquisition check, a segment and seat cut check, a product market fit scorecard, launch plans, customer interview guides, retention playbooks, partner programs, crisis playbooks, competitive battle cards and a CRAFT review of any GTM document. It is for founders and CMOs, and every tool works only from the details you give it.

Is there a free product market fit scorecard tool I can use in supported MCP clients?

Yes. The PMF Scorecard in CRAFT GTM takes your product, target market and metrics such as MRR, churn, NPS, CAC and LTV, and returns a product market fit score for each dimension, priority actions and the data gaps to fill.

What free tool builds a B2B launch plan or a competitive battle card?

In CRAFT GTM, Launch Commander builds a launch timeline, messaging for each segment, success metrics, risks and a prelaunch checklist. Competitive Intel builds a battle card for each competitor with objection handlers, a win and loss analysis and a quick reference card.

How do I use CRAFT GTM in supported MCP clients, and is it free?

CRAFT GTM is free to use, with no account, sign in or API key. Add https://craft-gtm.gtmhelix.com/mcp as a custom connector in Claude (web, desktop or mobile), in ChatGPT on plans that allow developer mode, or in Cursor, VS Code or Windsurf; in Claude Code, run claude mcp add --transport http craft-gtm https://craft-gtm.gtmhelix.com/mcp. You can also run the tools in a browser on this site.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the CRAFT GTM code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. What you type and the answer are not stored, and nothing you type is logged. Only an anonymous count of tool calls is kept. The privacy page has the details.

How to read the answer

Each tool returns a structured plan or scorecard built from fixed rules, such as a product-market fit score out of 10, a phased launch plan, or a competitive battle card. Where your inputs leave out a metric or fact, the output uses bracketed placeholders and labels any benchmark as an example figure to replace with your own.

What to do next

  • Founder: Start with PMF Scorecard or Customer Interview Kit to test whether your current segment is ready to scale before adding headcount.
  • CEO: Use PMF Scorecard, Partner Architect, and Crisis Planner to align leadership on product-market fit gaps, partner strategy, and risk response.
  • CMO: Run Launch Commander, Competitive Intel, and CRAFT GTM Analyzer to plan your next product launch, refresh battle cards, and check your five CRAFT dimensions.
  • CRO: Use Competitive Intel and Partner Architect to arm sellers against named competitors and structure co-selling motions.
  • Demand generation head: Use Launch Commander and Retention Playbook to coordinate launch campaigns and lifecycle expansion programs.

Why come back

Re-run these tools after a new batch of customer interviews, before each product launch, or at the end of a 90 day go to market cycle. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.
